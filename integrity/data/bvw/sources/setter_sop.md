**USING CLOSE CRM**

As a Setter, you will use Close CRM for the following:

  - Communication with leads (make dials and send SMS/emails to your leads)
  - Smart Views (your leads bucketed into sections for easy management)
  - Tasks (assign tasks/reminders for following up with your leads)
  - Opportunities (track your leads and their progress through the pipeline)
  - Lead Ownership (found in the Custom Fields section at the bottom left of a lead's profile)

[Click Here For Video Demonstration](https://www.loom.com/share/2eef516a1d3743dcac4196fbdcca14a4)

**BOOKING PROCESS**

Use the provided Calendly links to book calendar calls and live transfers. Ask your Sales Manager for the correct links if you do not have them already.

  - Round Robin link: access to all closers' calendars. Use this whenever scheduling a standard calendar call
  - Live Transfer links: these are individual closer calendars. Use these whenever scheduling a live transfer call
  - Add notes to each lead in Close CRM to give the closer the necessary context after booking
  - Send follow-ups and confirmation reminders for every single call that you book
