# Barak Van Wyk Coaching: closing playbook notes

Companion to `playbook_closer.json`. For each phase: one or two exact quotes from a won call showing the move done well, and why the phase is in the playbook.

## What this is built on

- 14 calls in `closer_all.json`. The 12 Zoom calls were read in full. The 2 Drive calls (recG83GHIMQ9gNUul, recuD9fhc8NSBGK9z) are still being transcribed, so they are not used as evidence either way.
- Won (Closed, payment type not Deposit or Instalment Paid): 2 of the 12.
  - recGhnRsvZefXBVSx: betrayed wife on her own. Pitched the 12-week individual program at $4,500 and closed on the $1,500 first month, paid in full on the call.
  - recCs5H4hJqOJtQip: man with a long pattern of serial cheating who calls himself a sex addict. Pitched the $4,500 program and closed on the $500 One Call Session.
- Closed on a first payment or deposit (supporting evidence only, no quotes): rec6IqrIcBNPKz9e9, recbpZf1niLOSFMfR, reckX0EYFDCGQ5w0o, recJJyTCIcSpWhIZE.
- Did not close (contrast set): rec1tlTKpupDVqmfF, rec6SUNdHWOTKNy5d, recK8cDNTYByeQw69, recPLMk8RaNuavRQv, recwyYh5D2HTXy3oB (Offer Made), recmKsZB35MoB68hY (Lost).
- Two strict wins is a thin base. Both are solo buyers, so moves that only happen with couples come from the first-payment closes. Re-check the playbook once the Drive calls are transcribed.

The order of phases is the order Makena runs them on every call. The structure itself barely changes between calls. What separates the wins is how hard she pushes in C5 and C9, and whether she offers a smaller first step and takes payment while the prospect is still on the call (C12, C13).

## C1 Open and frame (10)

> "My name's Makena, by the way, I help Barack with his onboarding application process, good to meet you." (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "Are you still good for around 45 minutes today?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

Evidence: every call opens this way. Two checks were added because their absence cost sales:
- **Money decision-maker on the call (C1d).** In recJJyTCIcSpWhIZE the wife handles the money and was not on, so the call ended with only a $100 deposit. In recwyYh5D2HTXy3oB the husband would not run the numbers on the call.
- **Say up front that a decision comes at the end (C1e).** In recK8cDNTYByeQw69 the prospect said he "wasn't expecting To be asked the obligating question" and that the meeting "was not clearly outlined". That surprise turned into his reason not to buy.

Also: rec6IqrIcBNPKz9e9 had to stop halfway because the prospect was taking the call at work, which is why C1b checks for a private, quiet spot. No call discloses that it is recorded. This is listed as a rule, not a check, because it did not affect any outcome.

## C2 Goal from each person (10)

> "Barak kind of filled me in, but what is your goal right now, either with yourself or with your relationship?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "So, I guess, for you guys, like, is the goal still to stay together, or where is he at right now with the relationship?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: both wins start with the prospect's own goal and then check whether they want to stay together. On couples calls Makena asks each partner in turn and asks the partner who cheated whether they want this "for yourself, too, not just for her" (recbpZf1niLOSFMfR, a first-payment close).

## C3 What happened and the pattern (10)

> "How long ago did the infidelity happen?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "And are you concerned that it's continuing, or it might continue on?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: on every call, how long it has gone on and whether it is a pattern become the anchors for the pitch later ("this has been happening your whole life"). On reckX0EYFDCGQ5w0o (a first-payment close) she stopped the couple's on-call fight ("I'm just gonna pause you guys for a second") and still closed. That is why C3e is there.

## C4 The why under it (10)

> "Yeah, so if you like the feeling that it gives you, why do you want to break this cycle?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "Well, that's why you're here, right? To figure that out." (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: in every call that closed, she asks what feeling they were chasing and then turns "I don't know" into the reason to work with Barak. Later, the pitch's "identify why you cheat" step depends on this answer.

## C5 Accountability and the honest mirror (15)

> "Yeah, can… so can I be honest with you, Kevin?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "Ghosting people, is that showing them respect?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "Yeah. I think that's an important thing to note, like, you're aware that you're not perfect either, however, it doesn't necessarily justify cheating at the same time." (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: this is the clearest difference between the calls. In recCs5H4hJqOJtQip she calls out the prospect's mixed signals three times and says Barak "can't necessarily help" without a real will to change. Shortly after, the prospect himself asks to pay for a session. In recPLMk8RaNuavRQv the husband kept falling back on the claim that his wife had given permission. His accountability was never settled, he stayed the unconvinced partner, and the couple did not buy. It carries a weight of 15 because the purchase decision follows from it.

## C6 What they tried and why now (10)

> "Have you done anything to try to, like, work on, like, the emotional regulation and be more present?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "Yeah, why do you feel like it didn't work for you?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "What did you see from Barack's content that made you feel like he could be the right fit for you in terms of the support that you need?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: most prospects have already tried therapy, a pastor or rehab. The wins ask why it failed, and the pitch then contrasts that with Barak: "not talk therapy, action". In recK8cDNTYByeQw69 she never asked what drew him to Barak. He later said he did not yet trust Barak and wanted to "interview" him.

## C7 Future picture and identity (10)

> "Who do you feel like that will help you become?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "Would you wanna be with someone though that wouldn't want the best for you and for you to be happy and healthy?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: in recGhnRsvZefXBVSx the prospect's real objection was fear that becoming happier would drive her husband away. Working through that, as part of the future picture, is what made the first-month yes possible.

## C8 Cost of staying stuck (10)

> "Have you thought about, like, what happens if… Nothing necessarily changes in the next 6 months" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "do you feel good about the way that you've been living your life now?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

Evidence: she asks the cost question on every call. In the wins it leads straight into the commitment question (C9). On the calls that did not close, the answer is left sitting there.

## C9 Commitment and belief check (10)

> "I mean, do you believe change is possible for you?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "Yeah, do you want that identity? Like, you're just not a relationship guy?" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "Do you feel like you're ready to actually like commit to yourself and the tools that you need to?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: in both wins she gets a spoken commitment to the work before or right after the price. C9d was added (best practice) because two couples were surprised by the price and did not buy. rec1tlTKpupDVqmfF: "This is the first time we've heard of any price". recPLMk8RaNuavRQv: Barak had told them "low to mid thousands". On rec6IqrIcBNPKz9e9 she did ask what Barak had already told the prospect about the investment.

## C10 Pitch: Barak's 12-week process (15)

> "this isn't, like, traditional therapy where you're just gonna talk and talk and talk, and then nothing is gonna be solved." (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "So, identifying why you cheat, trace the behavior back to its kind of emotional, developmental origins." (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "It's not gonna be an overnight thing, I'm not gonna promise that." (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

Evidence: the pitch content is almost the same on all 12 calls, so the pitch alone does not explain who buys. Its job is to tie the steps to what the prospect said: their kids, their self-trust, Rachel. It also always ends with "rebuild or separate with closure", which keeps it clear of outcome promises.

## C11 Investment and the commitment question (10)

> "So, with just one person, it's $4,500 total for the 12 weeks, and you can break it up if needed" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "And assuming that we can kind of figure out the financial side of things, is this something that you do want to move forward with?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "Finances aside, though, is this something that you feel can actually help you gain the self-trust back, the emotional regulation, and find more peace?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: she uses the "assuming we can figure out the financial side" question on almost every call. Asking "finances aside, will it help you?" separates belief from money before any discount is offered.

## C12 Objection and step-down (15)

> "Are you open to doing the first four sessions with Barack and seeing how they go and then deciding from there if this is something that you want to move forward with?" (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

> "I just don't know that, like, you thinking about it is necessarily gonna give you the answers as much as talking to him will." (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "And then if you do end up deciding to continue, you can just use that towards the rest of the program" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

Evidence: both strict wins are step-downs: the first month at $1,500 and one call at $500. Each was offered after the full program had been pitched and the hesitation turned out to be about certainty, not cash. On the calls that did not close, the step-down either never came or came after the prospect had already dug in:
- recwyYh5D2HTXy3oB and rec1tlTKpupDVqmfF: no first-month or one-call option, and the prospect left to "look at the budget".
- recK8cDNTYByeQw69 and recPLMk8RaNuavRQv: the step-down came late, after a long back-and-forth.

C12f (best practice) was added because the reconnects were left open. rec6SUNdHWOTKNy5d: "I will… Text you guys, next week", with a competitor call booked in between. recwyYh5D2HTXy3oB: "just send me a text". By contrast, rec1tlTKpupDVqmfF at least set a time for Wednesday.

> "Yep, you can book in his calendar, just make sure it's a time that you can commit to" (recCs5H4hJqOJtQip)

> "I'll share him, fill him in on what you told me today as well." (recGhnRsvZefXBVSx)

Evidence: every close, strict or partial, was paid while the prospect was still on the call. recGhnRsvZefXBVSx waited while she went upstairs for her card.
