# **KIMBERLY RAE — LOW-INTENT DM HANDOFF**

Move the lead out of Kimberly’s DMs if they are:

  - Giving very short/vague answers
  - Taking a long time between responses
  - Not asking or engaging much
  - Showing limited intent but there is still a potential problem
  - Unlikely to complete a full setting conversation through DM

**Do NOT continue forcing the entire DM script.**

### **Kimberly Handoff**

Okay, I think there could be a little more to unpack here.

Rather than trying to go back and forth forever over DMs, let me get someone from my team to reach out to you, learn a little more about what’s going on, and see if this is actually something we might be able to help you with.

What’s the best number to reach you on?

# **KIMBERLY RAE — PHONE SETTING SCRIPT**

## **Setter Introduction**

Hey \[Name\], it’s \[Setter\] from Kimberly Rae’s team. Kimberly asked me to reach out after you guys were chatting on Instagram.

Do you have a couple minutes?

### **Context Bridge**

Perfect. She gave me a little context, but I wanted to hear it from you directly.

She mentioned you’ve been dealing with \[brief problem from DM\].

What’s been going on?

### **Discovery**

What would you say is the biggest challenge with it right now?

**If vague:**

Would you say it’s more:

  - Situationships?
  - Finding someone who actually wants commitment?
  - Confidence?
  - Mixed signals?
  - Repeating the same relationship patterns?

### **Deep Dive**

And what does that actually look like for you?

How long has this been going on?

**Dig into specifics. Do not rush through questions like a checklist.**

### **Impact**

How has that been affecting you personally?

Has it affected your confidence, stress, or how you feel about dating in general?

### **Current Efforts**

What have you tried so far to fix it?

Therapy? Coaching? Courses? Trying to figure it out yourself?

And has any of that actually changed the problem?

### **Why Now?**

What made you want to figure this out now rather than just keep doing what you’ve been doing?

### **Future Consequences**

If nothing changes and a year from now you’re still dealing with the exact same thing, how would you feel about that?

### **Commitment Check**

So be honest with me — how committed are you to actually changing this?

Are you ready to put work into fixing it, or are you more at the stage of just exploring your options?

### **Soft Pitch**

Okay. Based on what you’ve told me, I can understand why Kimberly wanted us to connect.

This is the kind of thing she works with people on, but obviously I can’t tell you from this conversation alone whether her program would actually be the right fit for you.

The next step would be a proper consultation with our Client Success Manager. She can go much deeper into your situation, look at what’s actually keeping you stuck, and determine whether we can genuinely help.

Would you be open to that?

### **Financial Qualification**

Perfect. And just so we don’t waste your time, Obviously working with Kimberly does come with an investment depending on the level of support.

If everything made sense and you felt confident it was the right fit, are you currently in a position to invest in getting help with this?

If they ask how much: Again it depends on the level of support you guys decide on, it could be anywhere in the low-to-mid four figures. Does that sound doable?

### **Priority**

And from 1–10, how much of a priority is getting this handled for you right now?

**If below 7:**

What makes it a \[number\] rather than something higher?
