# **KIMBERLY RAE — RESPONSIVE DM SETTING**

### **Opening**

Hey \[Name\]\! Quick question, are you currently dating someone, stuck in a situationship, or just frustrated with dating in general?

### **Discovery**

What’s the biggest challenge you’re dealing with right now?

**If vague:**

Is it more:

  - Situationships?
  - Finding someone who wants commitment?
  - Confidence?
  - Mixed signals?
  - Repeating the same patterns?

### **ROUTING CHECK**

**Keep the conversation in Kimberly’s DMs if the lead is:**

  - Responding consistently
  - Giving thoughtful/detailed answers
  - Clearly experiencing a real problem
  - Asking questions or engaging back
  - Showing genuine interest in changing their situation

If they’re engaged → continue below as Kimberly.

If they aren’t then move into this [dialing script](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bx3VXnMb105fI6LDZ4MTEmXvb6qcVY-wJfPbd7kHI5w/edit?usp=sharing)

### **Deep Dive**

What does that actually look like for you day to day?

How long has this been going on?

### **Impact**

How is this affecting you outside of dating — your confidence, stress, or how you feel about your future?

Most people reach out because they’re exhausted from never feeling secure in where they stand.

### **Current Efforts**

What have you actually done to try and change this?

Have you tried therapy, coaching, courses, or mostly tried to figure it out yourself?

Has any of it actually worked?

### **Why Now?**

Why are you looking for answers now? What changed?

### **Future Consequences**

If nothing changes and you’re in this exact same place a year from now, how would you feel about that?

### **Commitment Check**

Be honest — are you actually committed to changing this, or are you hoping it eventually works itself out?

### **Soft Pitch**

Honestly, I work with a lot of people dealing with exactly this. They’re tired of wasting time and ending up in the same situations over and over.

I obviously don’t know yet if I’d actually be able to help with your specific situation. We’d need to have a proper conversation first to get more context and see if we’re a fit.

But do you think it would be helpful to have someone walk through everything with you and identify exactly what’s keeping you stuck?

### **Financial Qualification**

And just so we’re on the same page, working with me requires an investment depending on the level of support.

If everything felt like the right fit, is that something you’re in a position to do right now?

If they ask how much: Again it entirely depends on the level of support you guys decide on, but to be conservative it could be anywhere in the four figure range. Does that sound doable?

### **Priority & Timeline**

On a scale of 1–10, how much of a priority is fixing this for you right now?

### **Transition to Call**

Okay, I think the next step would be a call with my Client Success Manager.

She’ll get a full picture of where you’re at, what you’re struggling with, and determine whether working together would actually be the right fit.

What time zone are you in and what days work best?
