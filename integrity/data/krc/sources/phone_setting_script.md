# **Kimberly Rae Chapman: Phone Setting Script**

**Who runs this:** Setter, on a speed-to-lead dial to fresh opt-ins. **Goal:** book a qualified strategy call and lock the show. You are not selling the program. You are getting clarity, qualifying lightly, and pointing her to the right person.

**Speed rule:** Dial within 5 to 10 minutes of the opt-in. Always double dial (call, no answer, hang up, call again immediately). Only leave a voicemail and text after the second miss.

**Tonality:** This is dating and relationships, not B2B. Lead with warmth and real curiosity. Slow down, let there be pauses. You are a calm guide, not a salesperson. If you sound like a pitch, she shuts down.

## \-----**1. OPEN AND RAPPORT**

Confirm it is her, break the guard, anchor to the opt-in.

**Say:**

*"Hey, is this \[NAME\]?"*

(wait for yes)

*"Hey \[NAME\], it's \[SETTER\] calling from Kimberly Rae Chapman's team. How's your day going so far?"*

(let her answer, match her energy, keep it light for a beat)

*"Awesome. So the reason I'm reaching out... you just grabbed \[THE FREE TRAINING\] on \[attracting a quality, committed partner\], right? I wanted to make sure you actually got access to it okay. Sometimes those things get buried in the inbox."*

**If she sounds rushed or guarded:** stay warm and unbothered. *"No worries, I'll be super quick. I just want to make sure you got value out of what you grabbed."* Then move to the frame.

## **2. FRAME AND PERMISSION**

Get permission to ask questions. This earns you the right to dig.

**Say:**

*"Perfect. So while I've got you... part of what I do is make sure the women who grab Kimberly's training actually get pointed in the right direction, instead of it just sitting in their downloads."*

*"Mind if I ask you a couple quick questions about where you're at in your love life right now? That way I can see if there's actually a way we can help you, and if there is, get you connected with the right person on our team. Sound good?"*

Once she says yes, you are leading. Stay curious, not clinical.

## **3. MINI-DISCOVERY**

Isolate the real problem in her words, understand it, then flip to what she actually wants. Do not rush past the problem. Once you find it, put your shovel in the ground and dig.

**Isolate the problem:**

*"So when it comes to your dating and relationships right now... what's the biggest thing that's just not working the way you want it to?"*

**Probe and chunk down:**

*"Got it. When you say \[her words\]... what does that actually look like day to day? Walk me through it."*

*"And how long has that been going on for you?"*

**Background:**

*"Just so I understand where you're at... are you single right now, dating someone, recently out of something?"*

**Doubt:**

*"What do you think has been getting in the way of you having the kind of relationship you actually want?"*

**Solution:**

*"Have you tried anything to fix it so far? Apps, coaching, books, therapy, anything like that... and how did that go for you?"*

**Why now (keep this gentle, not a knife twist):**

*"Makes sense. So what made you reach for this training now... as opposed to six months ago, or just letting it ride another year?"*

*"And honestly... what's it been costing you, having this go unsolved? How does it spill over into the rest of your life?"*

**Desired situation (this is the turn, let her dream a little):**

*"Okay, let me flip it on you... if we fast forward and this was completely handled. The relationship, the partner, all of it. What does your love life actually look like? Paint me the picture."*

*"And how would that change things for you? How would you feel waking up in that?"*

## **4. PROPOSE THE CALL (Sell the Call First)**

Reflect her situation back, then sell the value of the call itself. Not the program. The call.

**Say:**

*"Okay \[NAME\], based on everything you just told me... \[reflect her exact situation back in one line, for example: you've been doing all the right things but keep ending up with the same kind of guy and you're tired of it\]..."*

*"This is honestly exactly the kind of thing Kimberly and her team help women through every single day. So here's what I'd love to do."*

*"I want to get you on a \[BREAKTHROUGH CALL\] with one of our coaches. It's about \[LENGTH\], completely focused on you, and they'll break down exactly what's been keeping you stuck and map out the actual path to fix it."*

*"Worst case, you walk away with total clarity. Best case, this is the call that finally changes it for you. Want me to grab you a time?"*

## **5. FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION**

She has agreed to the call. Before you book the time, softly confirm she can actually invest if it is the right fit. This hands the closer a pre-qualified appointment and kills sticker shock later. You are not collecting bank details or offering payment plans. That is the closer's job. Confirm willingness and resources, then book.

**Say (set it up honestly):**

*"Before I grab your time, can I ask you an honest question? I ask everyone this, so don't read into it."*

(soft pause)

*"The work Kimberly's team does is a real investment in yourself, and the women who get the biggest results are the ones who are genuinely ready to put some skin in the game to finally fix this. So if you got on that call and it was clearly the right fit for you... are you in a spot where you'd be able to invest in yourself to make it happen?"*

**Optional range anchor (only if you choose to anchor, swap in the real number):**

*"Just so nothing catches you off guard, the programs typically land somewhere in the \[RANGE\] range. I'm not asking you to commit to anything today, I just want to make sure it's even worth your time hopping on. Could you see yourself investing in that if it's right for you?"*

**If she says yes or absolutely:** Great. Move straight to Book and Confirm.

**If she hesitates ("it depends," "I'd have to figure it out," "not sure right now"):** use the resourcefulness reframe.

**Say:**

*"Totally fair, and I appreciate you being straight with me. Let me ask it this way... if this turned out to be the thing that finally got you \[WHAT SHE WANTS\], are you the type of person who'd find a way to make it work? Whether that's moving some things around, a plan, whatever it takes?"*

(if she is a yes here, move to Book and Confirm and let the closer handle the specifics)

**If she is a hard no ("I have no income and no way to invest anything"):** do not force a booking that turns into pressure.

**Say:**

*"I really appreciate your honesty, and I'd never want to put you on a call that turns into pressure when the timing isn't right for you. Here's what I'd suggest... keep working through \[THE FREE TRAINING\], and let's reconnect when things have shifted a bit. Sound fair?"*

(your call on whether to soft-book her for later or fully step back, based on the model)

**Warning:** Do not hand out payment plans, discounts, or pricing specifics on this call. Your only jobs here are to confirm she is serious, confirm she is resourced, and book the time. Everything financial beyond that belongs to the closer.

## **6. BOOK AND CONFIRM**

Offer two times, lock it, confirm the calendar invite while she is still on the phone.

**Say:**

*"Amazing. I've got \[DAY\] at \[TIME\], or \[DAY\] at \[TIME\]... which one works better for you?"*

(she picks)

*"Perfect, locking that in for \[DAY\] at \[TIME\]. What's the best email to send the invite to?"*

(repeat the email back to confirm it)

*"Sending it over right now. Do me a quick favor and accept that calendar invite while I'm still on with you, just so I know it's locked in on your end. Let me know when it pops up."*

**Do not move on** until she confirms she got it and accepted. A booking that is not on her calendar is a no-show waiting to happen.

## **7. LOCK THE SHOW**

The inferior frame plus a verbal commitment. This is what separates a 50 percent show rate from an 80 percent one.

**Say (get her word):**

*"Awesome. Hey, can you do me one quick favor... these spots are limited and our coaches actually block out real time to prep for these. So the only thing I ask is, if something comes up and you genuinely can't make it, just shoot us a message to reschedule instead of leaving them hanging. Can you give me your word on that?"*

(get the yes, this is the commitment that locks the show)

*"Perfect. And one more thing... come to the call somewhere quiet where you can actually focus. Not driving, not distracted. The more present you are, the more they can genuinely help you. Sound good?"*

**Say (set expectations and close warm):**

*"You'll also get a confirmation text and email from us. Keep an eye out and reply to the text so I know it came through."*

*"\[NAME\], I'm really glad you grabbed that training. I think this call's going to be a big deal for you. Talk soon, take care."*

## **8. OBJECTION BANK**

Stay relaxed. Most of these are reflexes, not real resistance. Acknowledge, reframe, move to book.

**"I don't really have time right now"**

*"Totally get it, I'll be quick, it's honestly like two minutes. I just want to make sure you actually get something out of what you grabbed. Cool if I ask you just a couple things?"*

*(if she truly can't) "No worries at all. When's a better time today or tomorrow I can catch you for two minutes?"*

**"Can you just email me the info?"**

*"For sure, I'll make sure you've got everything. The thing is, generic info is kind of why most people stay stuck. What actually moves the needle is a quick conversation so we can point you at what works for your situation specifically. That's exactly what this call is for. Want me to grab you a time?"*

**"What does it cost? / Is this free?"**

*"Great question. The call itself is completely free, it's a strategy session, not a pitch fest. They'll look at your situation and map out the path. If it makes sense to work together they'll walk you through what that looks like, and if it doesn't, you still leave with a plan. Fair enough?"*

(do not quote the program price on this call, move straight to booking)

**"I'm not sure this is really for me"**

*"Fair. Can I ask, you grabbed \[THE TRAINING\] for a reason, right? What were you hoping it'd help you with?"*

*(let her re-state the pain) "That's exactly what this call's built for. There's zero pressure and zero commitment. Worst case you walk away with clarity. Want to grab a time and just see?"*

**"I need to talk to my partner first"**

*"Totally makes sense. This first call is just to get clarity on your situation, there's no commitment to anything on it. Would it make more sense to have them join, or get clear yourself first and bring it back to them after?"*

## **9. NO ANSWER FALLBACK**

Double dial first. Only leave a voicemail and text after the second miss. Then work the cadence.

**Voicemail:**

*"Hey \[NAME\], it's \[SETTER\] from Kimberly Rae Chapman's team, calling about the \[TRAINING\] you just grabbed. Wanted to make sure you got access and see if I can point you in the right direction. I'll shoot you a quick text too. Talk soon."*

**Text:**

*"Hey \[NAME\], \[SETTER\] here from Kimberly Rae Chapman's team. Just tried calling about the \[TRAINING\] you grabbed. Did you get access okay? Happy to help you get pointed in the right direction. You around for a quick two minute chat?"*

**Cadence:** 2 to 3 dials in the first 2 hours while the lead is hot. After that, follow up across the trailing 7 days, working newest leads first and old leads last. Fight over fresh opt-ins, do not let them go cold.

## **10. CUSTOMIZE BEFORE YOU RUN IT**

Swap these brackets to match Kimberly's actual funnel and ICP. The structure stays the same, the language gets dialed in.

  - **\[THE TRAINING\]:** the exact name of her lead magnet (free training, guide, quiz, masterclass).
  - **\[attracting a quality partner\]:** the promise of that lead magnet, in her words.
  - **ICP language:** this draft assumes women seeking a committed partner. If she serves men, divorce recovery, post-breakup, or a different niche, swap the gendered and situational wording throughout.
  - **\[BREAKTHROUGH CALL\]:** what she calls the strategy call on the booking page.
  - **\[LENGTH\]:** actual call length (30, 45, 60 min).
  - **\[RANGE\]:** the real program price range for the financial qualifier, or remove the anchor entirely for lower price points.
  - **\[SETTER\]:** the setter's first name running the dials.
