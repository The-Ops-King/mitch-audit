# AI call review rubric: Barak Van Wyk Coaching: Closing Playbook (derived from calls)

Use on every recorded sales call and triage call. Score each check hit / partial / miss / not applicable, quote the evidence, and post the summary to Slack within an hour of the call.

## Hard flags (any one sends the call to a manager the same day)

- **Outcome promise**: Any guarantee or implied promise that the prospect will find a partner or save the relationship, including success-rate figures not in the script.
- **Off-sheet price**: Any price, discount, trial or payment structure not on the sanctioned price sheet: $5,400 weekly 1-hour session with Barak (both partners), couples exercises and individual homework between sessions; $4,500 weekly 1-hour 1:1 session with Barak plus homework; $500 one 1-hour session with Barak.
- **Credit or ID handling**: Rep asks for, hears read aloud, or types an SSN, date of birth or income figure, or suggests what to enter on a financing application.
- **Crisis not handled**: Prospect discloses self-harm, suicidal thoughts, abuse, violence or acute crisis and the rep keeps selling without care, a crisis line or a manager note.
- **No recording disclosure**: The rep doesn't say the call is recorded in the first minutes.
- **Pressure after a clear no**: The rep keeps pushing, shames, or invents urgency after the prospect clearly declines.
- **Therapy or credential claims**: Rep implies therapy, treatment, insurance coverage or a licence, or leaves the prospect believing so.

## Sales call: Barak Van Wyk Coaching: Closing Playbook (derived from calls)

Score = weighted share of applicable steps (hit 1, partial 0.5, miss 0).

### C1 Open and frame (weight 10)
- C1a: Introduces self and role ('I help Barak with his client application process')
- C1b: Confirms they still have about 45 minutes and a private, quiet spot (not driving, not at work)
- C1c: References what Barak or the setter shared, then asks to hear it from them first
- C1d: Confirms everyone who decides on the money is on the call, or gets agreement to decide without them
- C1e: Sets the agenda: understand the situation, see if Barak can help, and if so get the first session booked today (best practice: missing on his calls)

### C2 Goal from each person (weight 10)
- C2a: Asks what they want to achieve, for themselves or the relationship
- C2b: In couples, asks each partner separately and reflects each answer back
- C2c: Asks whether the goal is to stay together if there is a clear path to healing
- C2d: Asks the partner who strayed whether they want this for themselves, not just for the other person

### C3 What happened and the pattern (weight 10)
- C3a: Gets how long ago it happened and how it came out (told, found out, trickle truth)
- C3b: Asks whether it was once or a pattern, and how long the pattern goes back
- C3c: Asks whether it has stopped or the hurt partner fears it is continuing
- C3d: Asks the hurt partner how it felt to find out
- C3e: Keeps detail to what matters and pauses a couple who start fighting on the call

### C4 The why under it (weight 10)
- C4a: Asks what feeling they were after, or what void it filled
- C4b: Asks where it comes from (childhood, past relationships, self-worth)
- C4c: Challenges the easy answer ('if it were that simple you would have stopped')
- C4d: Normalizes not knowing why: that is what the work with Barak is for

### C5 Accountability and the honest mirror (weight 15)
- C5a: Asks permission to be honest, then names the mixed signals or justifications back to them
- C5b: Asks whether the behavior fits who they want to be ('is that how you want to be known?')
- C5c: Asks the strayer how it feels to hear the partner's hurt and whether they understand why trust is gone
- C5d: Holds the line that the partner's faults or the marriage's problems do not justify cheating, without judging
- C5e: States plainly that Barak can only help someone with a real will to change

### C6 What they tried and why now (weight 10)
- C6a: Asks what they have tried (therapy, pastor, rehab, self-help, nothing)
- C6b: Asks why it did not work or why they stopped
- C6c: Asks why now, after living with it this long
- C6d: Asks what in Barak's content made them think he is the right fit

### C7 Future picture and identity (weight 10)
- C7a: Asks the ideal outcome at the end of 12 weeks
- C7b: Asks who that will help them become
- C7c: Asks how that would feel, for each partner where both are on

### C8 Cost of staying stuck (weight 10)
- C8a: Asks what happens if nothing changes in 6 to 12 months
- C8b: Asks whether that is something they are willing to settle for
- C8c: Ties the cost to the people they care about (kids, partner, themselves)

### C9 Commitment and belief check (weight 10)
- C9a: Asks if they believe change is possible for them
- C9b: Asks if they are ready to commit to the work, regardless of how the relationship turns out
- C9c: Reassures: 'your situation is not inconsistent with the people we work with'
- C9d: Asks what they were told about the investment and corrects any mismatch before the pitch (best practice: missing on his calls)

### C10 Pitch: Barak's 12-week process (weight 15)
- C10a: Frames it as not traditional talk therapy: action, accountability, homework, only 12 weeks
- C10b: Walks the steps: find the why, trace it to its origins, repair impulse and validation systems, build new controls; healing for the hurt partner
- C10c: Ties the steps to the words they used earlier (their kids, their self-trust, their partner)
- C10d: Gives the end state: rebuild a secure partnership or separate with closure, not staying together at all costs
- C10e: Explains delivery: weekly 1-hour sessions with Barak plus work between sessions
- C10f: Makes no outcome promise ('not an overnight thing')
- C10g: Checks for questions or understanding

### C11 Investment and the commitment question (weight 10)
- C11a: States the full price for their program (5400 couples, 4500 individual) and that it can be broken up
- C11b: Asks 'assuming we can figure out the financial side, is this something you want to move forward with?'
- C11c: Separates belief from money: 'finances aside, do you feel this can actually help you?'
- C11d: Offers the monthly plan (1800 or 1500) when they cannot pay in one go

### C12 Objection and step-down (weight 15)
- C12a: Asks what is really holding them back and isolates it: have the money but unsure, have some, or a real stretch
- C12b: Works the real block (fear, identity, trust in Barak) with questions before touching price
- C12c: Points out that thinking it over will not answer it; meeting Barak will
- C12d: Steps down to the first four sessions or one call, credited to the full program
- C12e: Gets both partners' own yes before taking payment
- C12f: If they still need time, books a dated reconnect on the call rather than 'text me' (best practice: missing on his calls)

### C13 Take payment and book Barak live (weight 15)
- C13a: Sends the payment link by text or email while still on the call
- C13b: Stays on until the payment goes through, solving card, bank or fraud-hold problems live
- C13c: Sends Barak's onboarding and booking link and gets a session booked
- C13d: Asks them to pick a time they can commit to, within the next week
- C13e: Says she will brief Barak on what they shared and ends warm


## Triage / setter call: Barak Van Wyk Coaching: setter phone call

Score = weighted share of applicable steps (hit 1, partial 0.5, miss 0).

### S1 Open & anchor (weight 10)
- S1a: Confirms who they're speaking to and introduces self as Kimberly's team
- S1b: Anchors to what the lead did (application, quiz, training, DM)

### S2 Frame & permission (weight 10)
- S2a: Explains the purpose (point them in the right direction)
- S2b: Gets permission to ask a few questions

### S3 Discovery (weight 25)
- S3a: Biggest thing not working in dating/relationships
- S3b: Digs in: what does that look like, how long
- S3c: Relationship status / background
- S3d: What they've tried and how it went
- S3e: Doubt: what's getting in the way

### S4 Why now, cost & desire (weight 15)
- S4a: Why now
- S4b: Cost of the problem, spillover into life
- S4c: Desired picture if solved

### S5 Sell the call (weight 10)
- S5a: Reflects their situation back in one line
- S5b: Sells the consultation call (worst case clarity, best case changes it)

### S6 Financial qualification (weight 15)
- S6a: Asks if they can invest if it's the right fit
- S6b: Uses only the range anchor (low to mid four figures); no specific prices or plans
- S6c: Handles hesitation with the resourcefulness reframe or financing/credit check

### S7 Book & confirm (weight 10)
- S7a: Offers a live transfer or two specific times
- S7b: Confirms timezone and email
- S7c: Has them accept the calendar invite while on the phone

### S8 Lock the show (weight 5)
- S8a: Gets their word to reschedule rather than no-show
- S8b: Quiet place, present, not driving
- S8c: Connects them to the closer (intro text) and/or sends the pre-call video
