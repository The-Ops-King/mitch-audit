# AI call review rubric: BVW Master Closing Doc (completed from won calls)

Use on every recorded sales call and triage call. Score each check hit / partial / miss / not applicable, quote the evidence, and post the summary to Slack within an hour of the call.

## Hard flags (any one sends the call to a manager the same day)

- **Outcome promise**: Any guarantee or implied promise that the prospect will find a partner or save the relationship, including success-rate figures not in the script.
- **Off-sheet price**: Any price, discount, trial or payment structure not on the sanctioned price sheet: $5,400 weekly 1-hour Zoom session with Barak (together, sometimes each partner one-on-one), homework and couples assignments between sessions; $4,500 weekly 1-hour 1:1 Zoom session with Barak plus homework; the partner can join sessions later; $500 one 1-hour session with Barak.
- **Credit or ID handling**: Rep asks for, hears read aloud, or types an SSN, date of birth or income figure, or suggests what to enter on a financing application.
- **Crisis not handled**: Prospect discloses self-harm, suicidal thoughts, abuse, violence or acute crisis and the rep keeps selling without care, a crisis line or a manager note.
- **No recording disclosure**: The rep doesn't say the call is recorded in the first minutes.
- **Pressure after a clear no**: The rep keeps pushing, shames, or invents urgency after the prospect clearly declines.
- **Therapy or credential claims**: Rep implies therapy, treatment, insurance coverage or a licence, or leaves the prospect believing so.

## Sales call: BVW Master Closing Doc (completed from won calls)

Score = weighted share of applicable steps (hit 1, partial 0.5, miss 0).

### C1 Intro: rapport and frame (weight 8)
- C1a: Greets by name as 'from Barak's team' and checks they can hear okay
- C1b: States that everything discussed today is 100% confidential
- C1c: Discloses the call is recorded: if they work with Barak the notes go to him, if not it stays between the two of them
- C1d: Says some of this is personal and invites them to be completely transparent, the more honest the better she can see whether and how Barak can help ('Sound fair?')
- C1e: Says she has notes from their conversation with Barak but wants to hear it from them, then asks 'what's happened that's brought you here today?'
- C1f: Confirms they still have about 45 minutes and a private, quiet spot (added: not in script, from call evidence)
- C1g: Confirms everyone who decides on the money is on the call, or gets agreement to decide without them (added: not in script, from call evidence)

### C2 Discovery: understand what happened (weight 10)
- C2a: Lets them tell the story without interrupting too early
- C2b: Asks when it happened and how they found out
- C2c: Asks whether they were the one who was cheated on or the one who cheated
- C2d: Asks whether they are still together and what things have been like between them since it came out
- C2e: Probes only what the situation needs: one-time or ongoing, whether they know the full truth, any contact with the other person
- C2f: Probes only what the situation needs: separations, talk of ending it, kids, whether the partner wants to repair too

### C3 Isolate the real problem (weight 10)
- C3a: Asks 'what would you say is the hardest part for you right now?'
- C3b: Repeats their exact words back and asks what they mean by that, then unpacks it
- C3c: Asks 'is it mainly (their answer), or is there anything else making it difficult to move forward?'
- C3d: Cheated-on branch: asks about questioning the truth now, checking phone or whereabouts, replaying it, anger or resentment, and whether they feel emotionally safe
- C3e: Cheated branch: asks the hardest part since, whether the partner can hear them or everything comes back to what they did, guilt, whether they understand why they cheated, and whether they have done everything to rebuild trust
- C3f: Digs into the why under the cheating: what feeling they were after or what need it filled, challenges the easy answer, and frames not knowing why as what the work with Barak is for (added: not in script, from call evidence)

### C4 Current situation: dig in (weight 6)
- C4a: Asks them to walk through what the relationship looks like day to day (talking, fighting, sleeping separately, intimacy)
- C4b: Asks what normally happens when the cheating gets brought up
- C4c: Asks whether they are actually healing or 'having different versions of the same argument over and over again'
- C4d: Asks how long it has felt like this

### C5 What they've tried and the doubt question (weight 8)
- C5a: Asks what they have actually tried (therapy, couples counseling, books, space, talking it through themselves)
- C5b: Asks how it went and whether anything changed long term
- C5c: Asks the doubt question: 'what do you actually think is stopping the two of you from moving past this?' and listens without correcting

### C6 Why now and emotional cost (weight 8)
- C6a: Asks what made them decide to get help now, and why not keep trying to figure it out themselves
- C6b: Asks 'what's all of this actually been costing you?', not financially but personally
- C6c: Picks the relevant impacts (confidence, ability to trust, sleep, work, how they feel around their partner) without machine-gunning questions
- C6d: Cheated-on branch: asks what goes through their head when the partner is out, and whether they are becoming a version of themselves they don't recognize
- C6e: Cheated branch: asks what it is like wanting to move forward while everything is viewed through what happened, and how long they are willing to live like this

### C7 Future consequences (weight 7)
- C7a: Asks: if nothing changes and in 6 months or a year it's the same fights, mistrust and resentment, 'what happens to the relationship?'
- C7b: Stays silent after the question and lets them answer
- C7c: Asks whether they think they actually stay together
- C7d: Asks how they would feel if that is where it ended

### C8 Desired situation (weight 8)
- C8a: Flips it: if 6 to 12 months from now this went exactly the way they want, 'what does your relationship look like?'
- C8b: Asks what would need to be different for them to feel they had moved past the cheating
- C8c: Asks why saving or rebuilding this relationship is important to them and goes deeper
- C8d: Asks what trusting each other again and feeling close would give them back, and holds onto their exact words
- C8e: Asks each partner in turn and asks one how it feels to hear the other's answer (added: not in script, from call evidence)

### C9 Commitment (weight 10)
- C9a: Asks where their partner stands and whether the partner genuinely wants the same thing
- C9b: Asks them personally: if there was a clear process to work through this, rebuild trust and create the relationship they described, are they willing to do the work it takes?
- C9c: Gets a spoken commitment before moving on
- C9d: Asks the partner who cheated whether they want this for themselves, not only to keep the partner (added: not in script, from call evidence)
- C9e: Asks permission to be honest, names any mixed signals or justifications back to them, and says Barak can only help someone with a real will to change (added: not in script, from call evidence)

### C10 Tie down discovery (weight 2)
- C10a: Says she has a really clear picture now
- C10b: Asks if there is anything about what happened or where the relationship is that she should know and hasn't asked
- C10c: Asks what they were told about the investment and corrects any mismatch with the setter's 'low-to-mid four figures' before the pitch (added: not in script, from call evidence)

### C11 Transition (weight 4)
- C11a: Says this is exactly the kind of situation Barak works with
- C11b: Says she can see why what they have tried hasn't got them where they want to go yet
- C11c: Offers to walk through how Barak would get them from where they are to their desired outcome, in their own words, and then they decide
- C11d: Asks 'Fair?' and gets a yes

### C12 Pitch: high-level promise (weight 6)
- C12a: Says this isn't about pretending the cheating didn't happen or telling them to just 'forgive and move on'
- C12b: Names where couples get stuck: they stay together but the betrayal is still running the relationship
- C12c: States the goal: work through what happened, rebuild the trust, and stop living in their specific pain (suspicion, resentment, guilt, arguments, fear), so they get back to their desired outcome
- C12d: Names how Barak does it: a 12-week process, not traditional talk therapy that keeps you for years; action, accountability, homework, and Barak is direct (script blank: from won calls)
- C12e: Gives the end state: rebuild into an emotionally secure partnership, or consciously separate with closure; not staying together at all costs, either way they get their answer (script blank: from won calls)
- C12f: Uses the 'over 80% of couples stay together' stat only as a track record, never as a promise for them (script blank: from won calls)

### C13 Barak's method: the three pillars (weight 12)
- C13a: Pillar 1, find the why: identify why the cheating or lying happened and trace it back to its emotional and developmental origins (childhood, past relationships, self-worth) (script blank: from won calls)
- C13b: In Pillar 1, explains that until why the relationship is stuck is addressed, they will keep ending up in the same argument, and ties it to what they said ('Make sense?')
- C13c: Pillar 2, repair and rebuild: repair the impulse, attachment and validation systems for the partner who cheated; for the hurt partner, nervous system safety, clarity, emotional boundaries and self-trust (script blank: from won calls)
- C13d: In Pillar 2, uses their pain: 'you mentioned (their pain), so instead of (current reality) we're working toward (desired reality)'
- C13e: Pillar 3, make it last: build new internal controls so cheating becomes unnecessary and unattractive, plus personal authority and the tools for a healthier relationship, so it doesn't resurface in six months (script blank: from won calls)
- C13f: Closes the pillars with 'Does that line up with what you feel like you need?'
- C13g: For a couple, says Barak hears both sides, challenges both to take accountability, and finds a realistic middle ground (script blank: from won calls)

### C14 Delivery (weight 6)
- C14a: Duration and calls: 12 weeks, one 1-hour Zoom session with Barak each week, 12 sessions in all (some go bi-weekly) (script blank: from won calls)
- C14b: Couple vs individual: couples meet Barak together and sometimes one-on-one each; an individual can bring the partner into sessions later (script blank: from won calls)
- C14c: Resources between sessions: homework, weekly relationship-building exercises, individual reflection prompts and couples assignments (script blank: from won calls)
- C14d: Barak's involvement and support: Barak runs every session himself, is flexible on times for committed clients, and is reachable by email and phone between sessions (script blank: from won calls)
- C14e: Asks if they have any questions or if it all makes sense

### C15 Temperature check (weight 8)
- C15a: Asks how they feel about the process itself and the way Barak would work with them
- C15b: If uncertain, says what matters is that it's the right fit and asks for a 1 to 10 ('this isn't what I need' to 'exactly the help I've been looking for')
- C15c: If 8 or below, asks what is keeping them from a 9 or 10
- C15d: Resolves the belief gap before any price is given

### C16 Next steps: onboarding (weight 5)
- C16a: Explains what happens once they start: payment link and agreement form on this call, then Barak's onboarding link to book the first session, and she briefs Barak on what they shared (script blank: from won calls)
- C16b: Says they won't spend another few months trying random things; they start on their biggest problem and toward their desired outcome right away

### C17 Investment (weight 10)
- C17a: Recommends one program (Couples or Individual) because of a specific reason from discovery
- C17b: Frames it as getting them from their current pain to their desired outcome
- C17c: States the full price for that program: Couples $5,400 or Individual $4,500 for the 12 weeks (script blank: from won calls)
- C17d: Says it can be split into 3 monthly payments ($1,800 couples, $1,500 individual) (script blank: from won calls)
- C17e: Stops talking after the price
- C17f: Asks 'assuming we can figure out the financial side of things, is this something you want to move forward with?' (script blank: from won calls)

### C18 Objections (weight 12)
- C18a: First objection, always pace: takes money off the table and asks if, based purely on the process, this is genuinely what they need to reach their desired outcome
- C18b: If yes: 'so it's not whether you want to do this, it's how to make it work financially', then handles the money
- C18c: If uncertain: does not throw payment plans at them; asks 'what specifically are you unsure about?' and resolves the belief gap before money
- C18d: Isolates the money: have it but unsure, have some of it, or a real stretch; offers the 3-month plan for a real stretch (script blank: from won calls)
- C18e: Works the real block (fear of what change means, the partner, trust in Barak) with questions before touching price (script blank: from won calls)
- C18f: Answers 'I need to think about it' with: thinking about it won't give the answers that talking to Barak will (script blank: from won calls)
- C18g: Steps down to the first month (4 sessions) or a $500 One Call Session, credited toward the full program, only after the full pitch and only when the doubt is about certainty, not cash (script blank: from won calls)
- C18h: Gets each partner's own yes before taking payment (script blank: from won calls)
- C18i: If no payment on the call, books a dated reconnect on the call rather than 'text me' (script blank: from won calls)

### C19 Take payment and book Barak live (weight 10)
- C19a: Sends the payment link by text or email while still on the call ('typically we do it all on the call') (script blank: from won calls)
- C19b: Stays on until the payment goes through, solving card, bank or link problems live (script blank: from won calls)
- C19c: Sends the agreement form for the plan they chose (script blank: from won calls)
- C19d: Sends Barak's onboarding and booking link (the kickoff-session link in the doc) and gets the first session booked, at a time they can commit to within the next week
- C19e: Says she will brief Barak on what they shared and ends warm (script blank: from won calls)


## Triage / setter call: Barak Van Wyk Coaching: setter phone call

Score = weighted share of applicable steps (hit 1, partial 0.5, miss 0).

### S1 Open & anchor (weight 10)
- S1a: Confirms who they're speaking to and introduces self as Kimberly's team
- S1b: Anchors to what the lead did (application, quiz, training, DM)

### S2 Frame & permission (weight 10)
- S2a: Explains the purpose (point them in the right direction)
- S2b: Gets permission to ask a few questions

### S3 Discovery (weight 25)
- S3a: Biggest thing not working in dating/relationships
- S3b: Digs in: what does that look like, how long
- S3c: Relationship status / background
- S3d: What they've tried and how it went
- S3e: Doubt: what's getting in the way

### S4 Why now, cost & desire (weight 15)
- S4a: Why now
- S4b: Cost of the problem, spillover into life
- S4c: Desired picture if solved

### S5 Sell the call (weight 10)
- S5a: Reflects their situation back in one line
- S5b: Sells the consultation call (worst case clarity, best case changes it)

### S6 Financial qualification (weight 15)
- S6a: Asks if they can invest if it's the right fit
- S6b: Uses only the range anchor (low to mid four figures); no specific prices or plans
- S6c: Handles hesitation with the resourcefulness reframe or financing/credit check

### S7 Book & confirm (weight 10)
- S7a: Offers a live transfer or two specific times
- S7b: Confirms timezone and email
- S7c: Has them accept the calendar invite while on the phone

### S8 Lock the show (weight 5)
- S8a: Gets their word to reschedule rather than no-show
- S8b: Quiet place, present, not driving
- S8c: Connects them to the closer (intro text) and/or sends the pre-call video
